Hinge Counter 1.1.0 (8)

This update adds All history and four separate saved-count widgets: all-time, today, this week and this month. Manual entry is now a development-only tool and is compiled out of this production build. Existing manual entries, including restored backups, remain labeled and included in recorded totals. All history uses monthly or yearly chart totals for long spans and preserves every saved daily record; it has no rolling 90-day expiration.

The app is an independent offline utility. There is no login, account or in-app purchase flow. It uses the public UIKit UIHingeInteraction integration from the released 1.0.0 app; this update does not change the hinge adapter, angle thresholds or foreground-only observation policy.

Automatic counts require supported iPhone Duo hardware, iOS 27.1 or later, tracking enabled, and a complete close-to-open transition observed while an app scene is active. Locked, suspended, terminated or interrupted observations are not reconstructed. Opening the app on an already-open device does not add a count. Widgets read saved snapshots, do not monitor the hinge and refresh at iOS-controlled times. The app does not measure device-lifetime folds, hinge health or remaining durability.

Review steps:
1. Launch on iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later. Read the visible tracking-coverage notice. Today shows tracking status and the live hinge angle when available. Leave the app active through a complete close-to-open movement to exercise observation. Display/scene handoff can interrupt that movement and prevent a count.
2. On iPad and ordinary iPhones, use Try an unsaved demo to explore simulated movements. The demo is explicitly labeled, never changes saved counts, and does not imply a supported hinge. Production has no manual-add button.
3. Open History and switch among 7, 30, 90 days and All. With older saved history, All groups chart totals by month/year; Show older days exposes earlier daily records. A fresh install correctly has no historical observations.
4. Open Settings > Privacy Policy and Help & Support; these pages work offline. Export CSV/JSON. Restore only a throwaway test backup after replacement confirmation. Erase only test data after confirmation.
5. In the widget gallery, add All-time openings, Openings today, Openings this week and Openings this month. They display saved recorded totals; any historical manual entries remain identified. Weeks follow the device's first-weekday preference and month totals use Gregorian local dates through today.

Validation boundary: 51 Swift core tests cover history retention, source attribution, paging, calendar periods, time-zone travel, leap days, DST and snapshot rollovers. SDK builds and actual software UI results are recorded separately. No physical iPhone Duo qualification or new TestFlight hardware-runtime result is claimed. The product-page header is branded creative artwork derived from the existing icon; its symbolic 001 badge is not a screenshot or a claim about a recorded total.
