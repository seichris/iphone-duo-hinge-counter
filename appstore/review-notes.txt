Hinge Counter 1.1.2 (11)

This update adds a background Record Opening App Intent and a native link to Hinge Counter's page in Shortcuts. On iPhone Duo with iOS 27.2 or later, select Settings > Counting method > Shortcuts and turn on Shortcuts counting. Touch and hold Record Opening in Shortcuts and choose New Shortcut. In the editor's action library, choose Automation, then the iPhone opening/closing trigger. Select Opened only and allow immediate runs. Keep one opening-only automation enabled.

Each successful action invocation saves one Shortcuts entry, including a manual run. Foreground sensor observations, Shortcuts entries and historical manual entries remain separately labeled. The counting methods are mutually exclusive. Pause applies to both methods. The app's main-actor serial writer saves the authoritative archive before updating the UI or widgets. Existing version 1 archives are migrated while preserving totals and pause settings.

Reviewer checks
1. Open Hinge Counter on a physical iPhone Duo running iOS 27.2 or later. Choose Settings > Counting method > Shortcuts. Open the native Shortcuts link and confirm the Record Opening action is available.
2. Configure one opening-only automation using the steps above. With another app in front, close and open the phone. Confirm one Shortcuts entry per successful run. A close alone should add zero. Repeat while Hinge Counter is visible and confirm the observed subtotal stays fixed.
3. Pause Shortcuts counting and confirm the action reports that counting must be enabled. Re-enable it and confirm recording resumes. The app supplies manual setup instructions. A signed preconfigured import template is still pending.
4. Switch to Foreground sensor to exercise the existing live hinge path. A complete close-to-open observation is required while an app scene is active. Observation gaps reset continuity and earlier missed folds are not recovered.
5. Check History and the four saved-count widgets. Export CSV/JSON, and verify destructive actions require confirmation. Devices without a supported hinge can use the isolated unsaved demo.

Physical iPhone Duo qualification remains pending. The simulator omits the opening trigger from its Automation category and labels that trigger Unsupported Automation when previewing an existing signed opening-only workflow. Closing and reopening with Settings in front therefore left its saved subtotal unchanged. These results verify the hinge and the background action separately, and do not establish physical opening-trigger delivery, locked, post-restart or force-quit behavior.

The app is an independent offline utility with no login, account, ads, tracking or in-app purchases. It records app observations and explicit action runs. It does not measure device-lifetime folds, hinge health or remaining durability. Widget refresh timing is controlled by iOS.
