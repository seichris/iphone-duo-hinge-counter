Context for the September 23 rejection (submission 1de44519-7cf2-43c9-9fc4-789804ba5341): Apple announced on October 5 that iPhone Duo optimized apps can now be submitted: https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=kkphp5qo . This candidate requires Xcode 27.1/iOS 27.1 SDK declarations and directly uses public UIKit UIHingeInteraction; no dynamically constructed sensor API is used.

Hinge Counter is an independent offline utility for recording openings on a supported foldable device. It offers daily/all-time recorded totals, local history charts, manual entry, CSV export, JSON backup/restore and widget snapshots. There is no login or account and no in-app purchase flow.

Automatic observation is foreground-only. It does not keep running while locked, suspended, terminated or while another app is active; it does not reconstruct missed openings. Opening the app on an already-open device does not add a count. Widgets do not monitor sensors. The app does not measure hinge health or remaining hardware life.
