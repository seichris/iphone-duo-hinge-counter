Help & Support

Hinge Counter · updated 2026-09-21

Why does the count miss openings?

Automatic counting requires a supported hinge, tracking enabled, and a complete close-to-open movement observed while the app is active. Background, lock-screen and display-handoff interruptions can break observation. The app does not query a lifetime hardware counter. Add a clearly labeled manual entry when needed.

What counts as one opening?

The current detector requires an observed angle of 10 degrees or less followed by 170 degrees or more, at least 0.35 seconds apart. Partial folds do not rearm it. These are app thresholds, not an Apple specification or a hardware-durability test.

Why does my widget look old?

Widgets show saved snapshots and iOS controls refresh timing. They do not keep hinge monitoring alive. Open the app, check Settings > Widgets for a sharing error, and allow iOS to refresh. During testing, the app and extension must have matching App Group signing.

Back up, restore or erase

In Settings, export a JSON backup before replacing or erasing history. CSV is for reading daily totals, not restoring. Restore replaces history; it does not merge it. If storage fails, counting pauses: retry saving or loading, or export the original file for your own recovery. Never post that file publicly.

Report a problem

Use the public GitHub issue tracker below. Include the app version, operating-system version, device model, reproducible steps and whether the app was active. Remove personal information from screenshots. There is no need to upload your counter backup.

Contact the maintainer via a public GitHub issue. Do not post private information.
